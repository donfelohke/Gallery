## Curation rules

Before adding an asset, record:
- Title and intended use.
- Creator / source.
- Copyright holder and licence or written permission.
- Approval status and date.
- Brand domain and usage restrictions.
- Consent for people depicted, where required.

Do not infer permission from “publicly available.” Do not add private customer material, unlicensed work, drafts presented as approved, or stock material presented as real client results.

Maintain a living inventory in ASSET-MANIFEST.md when assets exist. Do not create empty category folders that imply a complete collection.

## Release rule

An asset is not approved until its original binary is committed, visual quality and mobile legibility are checked, source and rights are recorded, consent is confirmed where relevant, intended use and restrictions are clear, and a human review is recorded.
