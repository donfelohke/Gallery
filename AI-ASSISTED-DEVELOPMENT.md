# AI Assistance, Asset Rights and Provenance

**Status:** Proposed curation policy  
**Canonical policy:** [FELOH Corpus — AI-Assisted Development and Source Integrity](https://github.com/donfelohke/feloh-corpus/blob/main/AI-ASSISTED-DEVELOPMENT.md)

Gallery applies the canonical policy to photographs, illustrations, design files, maps, scans, audio/video and other curated media.

## Asset rules

- Record creator, original source, date, rights holder, licence or written permission, attribution, consent, restrictions, intended use and review status.
- AI-generated or AI-edited media must be labelled accurately; record the tool/model where known, source assets, material transformations and human editor.
- Do not present generated imagery as client work, documentary evidence, archival photography or an actual person/event.
- Never use AI transformation to disguise the origin of unauthorised artwork, photography, recordings or other restricted material.
- Public availability is not permission to redistribute or use commercially.
- Identifiable people, cultural materials and community-controlled works require appropriate consent and contextual review.
- Do not upload private client images, unreleased campaign material or restricted archival assets to external AI services without authority.
- Approval is specific to the named use; a campaign approval does not imply unrestricted redistribution.

## Licence boundary

The root LICENSE is not a blanket licence for every image, recording, artwork or other asset. The repository's software/documentation licence and each asset's rights are separate questions. Use the asset manifest as the rights record; where unclear, mark RIGHTS-UNCLEAR and withhold publication.

The NAKEBUSEN website remains text-first and must not embed or depend on Gallery assets.


## Asset transformation is not rights clearance

AI upscaling, inpainting, recolouring, tracing, vectorisation, cropping, style transfer, captioning or format conversion does not erase the source asset's authorship, licence, consent or restrictions. Record original asset identity and source, creator/rightsholder, permission/licence, transformation tool/model, material edits, editor, intended use and review status. Never use a transformed copy to conceal an unauthorised source or remove attribution/watermarks. If source rights are uncertain, mark RIGHTS-UNCLEAR and withhold both original and transformed derivatives from publication pending review.


For Gallery, **code laundering** includes transforming an unauthorised photograph, illustration, recording, design or other asset with AI or manual editing and then presenting the derivative as original or rights-cleared. Transformation does not remove the original asset's authorship, licence, consent or restrictions.
