# Gallery instructions for contributors and coding agents

## Purpose and authority
- Gallery is a curated asset repository, not an upload dump.
- The FELOH Corpus is the canonical source for shared ecosystem terminology and governance; this repository owns asset-level provenance and approval records.
- The public NAKEBUSEN website must remain graphic-free. Gallery assets may be linked as a separate destination but must not be embedded in website HTML or copied into the website repository.
- A file's presence, generation, submission, or public availability is not evidence of ownership, licence, consent, or approval.

## Asset safety and review
- Before proposing an asset, identify the creator/source, rights holder, applicable licence or written permission, intended use, relevant brand, consent needs, restrictions, and approval status.
- Use the states in CONTRIBUTING.md consistently: Proposed, Under review, Approved, Rejected, Retired.
- Mark unknown facts as UNKNOWN. Never infer permission from public availability.
- Preserve original masters unchanged. Keep derivatives distinct and traceable to a verified source.
- Do not present generated references, drafts, or third-party material as canonical brand originals.
- Do not commit private customer data, sensitive metadata, credentials, or media without an approved basis and permission.
- Do not mark an asset approved merely because a pull request is merged. Approval must be explicit and scoped to a stated use.

## Change control
- Read README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, ASSET-MANIFEST.md, and ASSET-APPROVAL-WORKFLOW.md before changing curation rules.
- Keep changes focused and use a descriptive branch and pull request.
- Update the manifest alongside each asset addition or replacement.
- Record what was checked, what remains unknown, and the intended publication context.
- Do not merge, publish, or claim rights/consent verification without evidence.
