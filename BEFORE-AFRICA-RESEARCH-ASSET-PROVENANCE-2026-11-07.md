# Before Africa — Research Asset and Provenance Standard

**Gallery research note · 7 November 2026 · Status: POLICY / RESEARCH**

## Purpose

Any image, map, manuscript scan, artefact photograph, archival record, performance recording or illustration used in the Before Africa research programme must carry provenance and contextual metadata. The presence of an image online does not establish permission to reuse it.

## Required record

- Asset title and stable source URL or catalogue identifier.
- Creator, photographer, artist, archive, museum or rights holder where known.
- Date or date range; distinguish creation date from digitisation/publication date.
- Place, community, language and cultural context, with uncertainty recorded.
- Object/site identification and catalogue number where applicable.
- Original caption and any edited caption, with translation attribution.
- Copyright/licence, reuse terms and access date.
- Consent status for living people and community-controlled material.
- Cultural restrictions, sacred or sensitive status, and permitted uses.
- Research claim the asset supports and limitations of what it can show.
- Review status: UNREVIEWED, RIGHTS-CHECK REQUIRED, APPROVED, RESTRICTED or REJECTED.

## Interpretation safeguards

Do not infer ethnicity, identity, date, function, ritual meaning or community ownership from appearance alone. Do not caption a historical image with a modern ethnic label unless the source supports it. Distinguish a contemporary reconstruction from an archaeological object or archival photograph. Never present generic stock imagery as documentary evidence.

## Recommended repositories and research gateways

- UNESCO, *General History of Africa*: https://www.unesco.org/en/general-history-africa
- UNESCO Digital Library, *General History of Africa I*: https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000042225
- Museum and archive catalogues should be searched by object/site identifier and their own rights statements. Record the institution's catalogue record, not only a third-party image host.

## Release gate

An asset brief is not approval. An image can be used in research notes only within the applicable licence and attribution terms. Public campaign use requires a separate rights and consent review. The website remains independent of Gallery assets and must not embed or depend on this repository.
