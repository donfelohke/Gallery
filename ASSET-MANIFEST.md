## Release checklist

Before marking an item approved:

- [ ] Original binary is committed under a stable, repository-relative path.
- [ ] File format, dimensions, visual quality and mobile legibility are checked.
- [ ] Text, spelling, prices, contact details and brand hierarchy are checked.
- [ ] Creator, source/provenance and copyright holder are identified as far as known.
- [ ] Licence or written permission covers the intended use; ambiguity blocks release.
- [ ] Required model/client/photographer consent and attribution are documented.
- [ ] Intended use, restrictions, approval authority and review date are recorded.
- [ ] Generated or illustrative imagery is not represented as real client work.
- [ ] Website's text-only / no-embedded-Gallery-images rule remains intact.
