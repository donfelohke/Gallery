## Do not prioritise

- The full strategy board as a final poster: it is a dense planning sheet and contains multiple mockups.
- Duplicates, composite sheets, unverified marks, or tiny text-heavy designs.
- Generic luxury, excessive glow/glitch effects, or unverified client-result imagery.
- Posters that advertise braiding; the current service scope excludes braiding.
- Unconfirmed offers, availability, prices, locations or claims.

## Asset admission gate

Before copying a source into `assets/`:

1. Retrieve the original image file and confirm it is the intended individual asset, not merely a description or composite board.
2. Check visual quality, text accuracy, dimensions and mobile readability.
3. Record the creator, source/provenance, rights basis, any people/photography consent, intended use and restrictions in `ASSET-MANIFEST.md`.
4. Mark as **proposed** until a human review explicitly approves it. Missing or ambiguous rights block publication.
5. Keep website pages text-only with a link to this Gallery repository; do not embed these images into the website repository.

## Recommended first production batch

Start with **A1 Arrival, A2 Craft, A3 Identity and A4 Care**. This covers awareness, proof, emotional positioning and retention without overfilling the collection. Add A5–A7 only after the individual source files and rights are verified.
