# GENJE ZA WARENA Launch — Asset Curation Brief

**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** Proposed intake and review brief; no assets approved by this document  
**Campaign:** GENJE ZA WARENA — The Fire of the Formidable

## 1. Source set

The campaign direction references seven supplied images and the website as an eighth governing reference.

- One authentic founder portrait: yellow cap and blue shirt. Intended role: dominant human anchor.
- Six other supplied images: supporting visual references whose exact motifs and roles must be assessed individually from the original files.
- Website: governing reference for current public message, brand hierarchy, project relationships and destination. It is not a Gallery image asset.

The image descriptions above do not establish that the original binary files are present in this repository. No files are approved or released by this brief.

## 2. Asset intake

For each of the seven images, create an individual manifest entry after the original source file is located. Record:

- Candidate ID and stable repository path.
- Original filename and source.
- Creator / photographer / rights holder.
- Licence or written permission for the intended campaign use.
- Consent where people are depicted.
- Intended role in the poster.
- Crop, retouching or derivative history.
- Approval state, reviewer and date.
- Restrictions and checksum where useful.

Do not infer permission from prior upload, public availability or use in a previous conversation.

## 3. Visual hierarchy

The founder portrait is the primary image and must remain recognisable. The other six images should contribute motifs, textures and supporting visual language; they should not become seven competing hero images or an undifferentiated collage.

The website governs the poster's messaging and destination. The poster must direct the audience to the website while remaining visually coherent and legible at mobile size.

## 4. Brand context

- **NAKEBUSEN:** Beautiful Roots. Sovereign Systems.
- **Supporting proposition:** Craft, technology and culture rooted in African ingenuity.
- **GENJE ZA WARENA:** The Fire of the Formidable — an adopted creative interpretation, not a verified universal etymology.
- **Founder / author:** Felix Lwova · Don Feloh · #HisGenjeness · @donfelohke.
- **Creative principle:** Penye NIA, Pana Njia.

GENJE is a cultural and creative identity, not a separate commercial checkout. NAKEBUSEN remains the only commercial interface. WARENA is the distinct systems-engineering identity.
