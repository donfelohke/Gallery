# Gallery rights and publication update — 2026-11-22

**Role:** Supporting asset governance, provenance, consent and approval. Gallery is not a practice domain.

## Publication rule

The public website remains text-first and may link to Gallery; it does not embed Gallery images or depend on assets to render. Public availability, possession or a repository commit is not permission to publish.

## Required asset record

For each candidate asset, preserve a stable ID, exact filename and hash, creator/source, capture or publication date, rights basis, attribution, consent where relevant, permitted uses, restrictions, intended destination, reviewer and decision date.

## State model

Use INTAKE, NEEDS-SOURCE, RIGHTS-UNCLEAR, CONSENT-PENDING, REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED and SUPERSEDED. Only APPROVED assets may be used for the recorded intended purpose. Unknown rights remain unapproved. Code/text licensing does not automatically cover third-party photographs, artwork, recordings or client material.

## Review sequence

1. Match manifest entries to actual committed binaries and hashes.
2. Confirm creator/source and licence or written permission.
3. Confirm participant consent and scope where people are identifiable.
4. Record intended use, attribution, restrictions and withdrawal route.
5. Human-review context, crop, representation and brand fit.
6. Publish only after approval; retain rejected and superseded history.

No image, portrait, campaign brief or thumbnail is approved merely because it exists in this repository. **ONLY NAKEBUSEN TRANSACTS.**
