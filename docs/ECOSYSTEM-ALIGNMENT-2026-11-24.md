# Gallery — Ecosystem Alignment Update

**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Role:** Supporting asset governance · **Status:** Asset inventory and formal approvals remain pending unless individual records state otherwise.

## Purpose

Gallery is the curated asset-provenance repository supporting FELOH, NAKEBUSEN, WARENA, IKAI and GENJE. It is not a practice domain, a public image CDN or an unreviewed upload dump.

## Identity and ownership boundaries

- FELOH Corpus owns shared identity definitions, terminology, evidence and provenance policy.
- NAKEBUSEN owns ROOT business communications and is the sole commercial transaction interface.
- WARENA owns technical systems documentation.
- IKAI owns reflective-computing research and participant-facing privacy requirements.
- GENJE owns creative practice and work-level credits.
- Gallery owns asset-level source records, rights, consent, review and approval state.

“GENJE ZA WARENA | NAKEBUSEN” is a creative/public signature, not an asset licence or approval.

## Required per-asset evidence

For every proposed asset, record creator, original source, rights holder, licence or written permission, intended use, distribution context, depicted-person consent where applicable, attribution, restrictions, expiry, file integrity, review outcome and explicit human approval. Use the existing states: Proposed, Under review, Approved, Rejected and Retired.

Public availability, generation, submission, repository presence or a merged pull request does not prove ownership, licence, consent or approval. Preserve original masters unchanged and keep derivatives traceable to their sources. Do not publish private customer media or identifiable photographs without a documented lawful and appropriate basis and consent.

## Publication boundary

The public website remains text-first and graphic-free under the current repository policy. Gallery assets may be linked as a separate destination where appropriate, but must not be embedded in website HTML or copied into the website repository. Gallery is not a public image-hosting dependency.

## Maturity and status

A brief is not an approved asset. A manifest entry is not a rights determination. A public URL is not consent. A pull request merge is not approval. Approval must be explicit and scoped to a stated use and distribution context.

## Next documentation priorities

Keep ASSET-MANIFEST, ASSET-APPROVAL-WORKFLOW, CURATION-QUEUE and the review-record template aligned. Reconcile each candidate asset against source, rights, consent and intended-use evidence before changing its status.
