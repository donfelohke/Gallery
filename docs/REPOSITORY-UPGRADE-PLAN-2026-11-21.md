# Repository upgrade plan — 2026-11-21

**Role:** Supporting asset governance, provenance, consent and release approval. Not a practice domain or public image dump.

## Non-negotiable policy

The public website remains text-first and may link to Gallery; it must not embed Gallery images or depend on these assets to render. Public availability is not permission. GPL coverage of repository code/text does not automatically grant rights to separately copyrighted assets.

## Upgrade workstreams

1. Every candidate asset has a stable ID, filename/hash, creator/source, capture date if known, rights basis, attribution, permitted uses, restrictions and approval status.
2. Document consent for identifiable people and limits on reuse; do not infer consent from possession or public posting.
3. Distinguish INTAKE, NEEDS-SOURCE, RIGHTS-UNCLEAR, CONSENT-PENDING, REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED and SUPERSEDED. Only APPROVED assets may be used for the recorded intended use.
4. Verify original files are present, readable and match the manifest; a brief or thumbnail is not approval evidence.
5. Review crop, resolution, mobile legibility, context and brand fit without misrepresenting people, communities or work.
6. Preserve contextual metadata and source lineage for Before Africa and other knowledge projects.
7. Keep website use to text links under current policy; no hotlinking or automatic gallery rendering.

## Release gates

No publication without a rights basis, consent where relevant, intended-use approval and human review record. Unknown rights means not approved. Keep rejected and superseded records auditable.

## Immediate sequence

1. Reconcile the manifest against committed binaries.
2. Resolve the exact supporting-image count in the GENJE campaign brief.
3. Audit approval states and source records.
4. Add deterministic manifest validation where feasible.
5. Publish an approval report without exposing private client information.
