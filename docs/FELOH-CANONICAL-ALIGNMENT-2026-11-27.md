# Gallery — FELOH Canonical Alignment · 27 November 2026

Gallery remains a **supporting asset-governance repository**, not a practice pillar, public image CDN, portfolio, business or transaction surface.

The shared philosophy is **BLACK EXCELLENCE — GRACE · GRIT · GLORY**:
- **GRACE / NAKEBUSEN:** beauty, heritage, care, loc craft and dignity.
- **GRIT / WARENA:** technical capability and sovereign systems.
- **GLORY / GENJE:** creative expression through sound, code and movement.

**FELOH / Don Feloh** remains the human author and decision-maker. IKAI remains reflective-computing research. NAKEBUSEN remains the sole customer-facing transaction interface.

## Asset implications

Campaigns such as **NAKEBUSEN LOCS · #BLACKPEARL** must preserve diverse Black beauty and loc expression; do not encode one phenotype, hair form, skin tone, gender presentation or styling as the sole standard of beauty. Cultural references require contextual accuracy and attribution.

For every image, recording, portrait, performance, score, movement capture or derivative, preserve:
- creator and source attribution;
- licence / ownership basis and permitted uses;
- identifiable-person consent and scope where applicable;
- cultural/editorial context and restrictions;
- original-to-derivative lineage and transformations;
- approval decision, reviewer, date, channel and expiry/review conditions.

Generated or uploaded material is not automatically approved for publication. A public source is not automatically reusable. A repository commit is not publication permission. The public website must not embed Gallery assets without explicit asset-specific approval.
